About

Good engineering is mostly good judgment: what to build, what to leave out, and what to make boring on purpose.

I work across the whole stack: React and Next.js on the front, Go, Ruby on Rails, Node.js and Kotlin behind it. That range lets me follow a problem to wherever it really lives, whether that's a screen, a queue or a payment flow.

I've built for creators at Teachable, for high-volume Pix and boleto payments at Cora, and led a small team shipping client work. I care about small decisions that compound: a clear name, a well-shaped API, a system that's easy to observe when it misbehaves. Before software, I spent a season in Iceland leading environmental projects with international volunteer groups, which taught me to work well with people from anywhere.
